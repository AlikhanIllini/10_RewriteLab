How It Works

1. Your query is converted into a 384-dimensional vector using the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embedding model.
2. All session texts (originals + rewrites) are also embedded.
3. Cosine similarity is computed between your query and each text.
4. The top matches are returned, deduplicated by session.

This approach was evaluated in Assignment 8 across three embedding models and three chunking strategies. all-MiniLM-L6-v2 was selected for its strong balance of speed and retrieval quality.
